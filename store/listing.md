## Name

**Sachet – Phishing X-ray**

(Store names are unique per listing: "PhishLens" was already taken by a live
listing, which is why the product was renamed. "Sachet" is the Nepali word
for "alert".)

## Short description (max 132 characters)

Shows why a login page looks fake, in plain Nepali or English, before you type your password.

## Detailed description

> Phishing pages copy the real login screen — same logo, same colours, same
> words. The only difference is the address, and that is exactly what most
> people never check.
>
> Sachet is a Phishing X-ray. Click the icon on any login page and it tells
> you, in plain English or Nepali, whether the page looks fake — and why:
>
> - This domain imitates eSewa's official site but is spelled differently.
> - The login form sends your password to a different server.
> - This page asks for an OTP and mentions Khalti, but it is not Khalti's site.
> - This page uses pressure language: "your account will be blocked".
>
> Verdicts are red (looks fake — do not log in), yellow (suspicious —
> proceed carefully), green (no warning signs found, on a verified official
> domain only), or gray (unknown site — no claim made). Sachet never says a
> site is "safe": phishing pages can pass checks, so green means no warning
> signs were found.
>
> Built for Nepal first: verified Nepali wallets and banks (eSewa, Khalti,
> IME Pay, Nabil Bank, NIC Asia Bank, Global IME Bank), with verdicts and
> explanations in natural Nepali, and detection of scam phrases in
> Devanagari and Romanized Nepali. You can also paste a suspicious link
> into the popup and check it before opening it — only the address is
> checked, the page is never visited.
>
> Private by design: every check runs on your device. No URLs, page text, or
> anything you type ever leaves your browser. No account, no tracking, no ads.
> Open source — the code and the brand list are public for anyone to inspect.
>
> Permissions are minimal: the extension only reads the page when you click
> the icon. It never asks for your browsing history or cookies.
